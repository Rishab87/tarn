An AI that lives on your Mac.

Tarn picks the right open AI for your Mac, downloads it, and runs it right here. No account, no subscription, nothing sent to the cloud.

Download for Mac

Free. For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 26 or later. The app is 16 MB; it downloads its AI after you open it.

Chooses the right AI for your Mac

When you first open Tarn it looks at your Mac’s chip and memory, then downloads the most capable model that still leaves half your memory free, so your browser and apps stay quick. You never have to know what a model is called.

Tarn checks for improved versions each time it opens, and better models arrive with app updates.

What it does

Private by default
Your chats stay on your Mac. No account and no analytics.
Looks things up
Searches the web for news, prices and anything recent, and links its sources.
Works in your folders
Share a folder and Tarn can read, organise and write files there. It asks before every change.
Connectors and skills
Plug in tools through MCP, and teach it how you like things done. Skills made for Claude work too.
Gives memory back
If your Mac runs low, Tarn offers a lighter model or frees its memory until you need it.
Moves with you
Back up every chat to one file and bring it to your next Mac.

Water you don’t spend

Cloud AI uses water to cool data centers and to make the electricity they run on. Every answer Tarn writes on your Mac is one a data center didn’t, and Tarn keeps count.

How we count it

Tarn counts 1.1 mL per answer. Google measured a typical chatbot answer at 0.26 mL of cooling water and 0.24 Wh of energy; generating that electricity uses about 0.9 mL more at the US-average rate. Your Mac’s own electricity isn’t subtracted. Sources: Google, 2025 and UC Riverside.

Opening Tarn the first time

Tarn is new and isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it the first time.

  1. Open Tarn.dmg and drag Tarn into Applications.
  2. Open Tarn. When macOS says it can’t verify Tarn, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Tarn.
  4. Confirm with your password. From then on Tarn opens normally.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no subscription.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer, including MacBook Neo) running macOS 26 Tahoe or later, with at least 8 GB of memory. Intel Macs are too slow for it.

How much space does it need?

The app is about 40 MB. The AI it downloads is 1.3 to 18 GB depending on your Mac: 5.7 GB on a 16 GB Mac.

Is it as smart as ChatGPT or Claude?

For everyday writing, summarising, planning, explaining and searching, it does the job well. For the hardest reasoning and very long documents, the big cloud models are still ahead.

What leaves my Mac?

Only three things: the one-time AI download from Hugging Face, web searches through DuckDuckGo when web search is on, and update checks. Your chats and files never do. Privacy details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the AI is downloaded, everything except web search works without internet.

How do I move to a new Mac?

Settings → Chats & Backup → Back up. On the new Mac, choose “Restore a backup” when Tarn first opens.

How do I remove it?

Quit Tarn, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Tarn, which holds your chats and the AI model.
