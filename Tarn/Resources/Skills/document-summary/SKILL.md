---
name: document-summary
description: Summarize long documents, PDFs, articles, contracts, reports or meeting notes, and pull out what matters.
---

Summarize so the reader can act without reading the original.

1. Start with a one-sentence bottom line: what this document is and the single most important takeaway.
2. Then "Key points": 3 to 7 bullets, most important first. Use the document's own numbers, names and dates.
3. Add only the sections that apply:
   - "Decisions and deadlines" for dates, amounts, obligations.
   - "Action items" with who does what by when, for meeting notes.
   - "Watch out for" for unusual terms, risks, fees, auto-renewals or anything that looks one-sided in contracts and agreements.
4. Quote short exact phrases when wording matters (legal or money terms).
5. If the document was cut off, say which part you saw.
6. Don't add facts that aren't in the document. If something important seems missing, say so.
7. Finish by offering to explain any part in simpler words.
